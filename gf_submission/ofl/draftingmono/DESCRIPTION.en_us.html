Drafting Mono is an original monospaced font made by indestructible type. It aims to capture the spirit and logic of typewritten alphabets, rather than mimic the appearance literally. It is a celebration of the production stage of projects, which often use monospaced fonts as a placeholder.

This font family includes a full range of weights, italics, an extended character set, and some OpenType features. It was made with love and will continue to improve with your support. Visit the website for more information! The project is developed on GitHub.
