Drafting Mono SC is the small caps companion to Drafting Mono: lowercase letters are set as small capitals, while capitals, figures and symbols stay the same. It shares Drafting Mono's full range of weights and italics.

Drafting Mono is an original monospaced font made by indestructible type. It aims to capture the spirit and logic of typewritten alphabets, rather than mimic the appearance literally. It is a celebration of the production stage of projects, which often use monospaced fonts as a placeholder. Visit the website for more information! The project is developed on GitHub.
